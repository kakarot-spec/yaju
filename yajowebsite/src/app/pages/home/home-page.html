ABOUT YAJU ACHARYA

A life in
music.

I'm Yaju Acharya, a flutist and musician from Bhaktapur, Nepal, whose connection to music began in early childhood. Encouraged by my mother, I stepped into the world of melodies and rhythms with great enthusiasm. What began as a gentle push soon became a personal calling, and my curiosity and love for music led me to explore various instruments and genres with unwavering dedication.

Rooted in Eastern Classical Music, with a growing desire to explore beyond traditional boundaries, I earned a Higher Diploma in Jazz Music from the Kathmandu Jazz Conservatory. This experience opened me up to a world of musical fusion and creative exploration.

Today I write music hoping that my music will be voice for people who have been hiding from their inner voice blending eastern classical and nepali folk genres together with an influence of western music. I write music about stories - stories of joy, stories of pain , stories of dreams.
